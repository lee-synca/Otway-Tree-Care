Frequently Asked Questions

Honest Advice. Expert Answers.

Removing a large or hazardous tree raises a lot of questions — permits, costs, safety, and what happens afterward. Here are the answers we give every day.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Colac or the Otways?

This depends on your specific overlay and the size/species of the tree. As part of our service, we can help identify if your tree falls under local council vegetation protection overlays — and if a formal arborist report is required, we can provide that too.

Will your heavy machinery damage my lawn or driveway?

We use specialised, low-impact machinery designed to distribute weight evenly. For sensitive areas, we use ground protection mats to ensure we leave your property exactly as we found it—just without the hazardous tree.

How do you handle trees near powerlines or houses?

This is our specialty. We use advanced rigging systems and "piece-down" techniques to dismantle the tree in small, controlled sections. Every cut is engineered to ensure a safe, vertical drop away from your assets.

Is your quote a fixed price or an estimate?

We provide fixed-price quotes. Once Tom assesses the job on-site, the price we give you is the price you pay—no hidden "complications" or surprise fees at the end of the day.

Are you fully insured and safety compliant?

Yes. We are fully systemised with professional SOPs and safety documentation (SWMS) managed through Standard Operating Systems. Our team cannot begin a job until all safety protocols are digitally verified.
