Who We Are & Why It Matters

"I started Otway Tree Care because I wanted to bring something different to this region — honest advice, qualified expertise, and a team that actually cares about the result. Having grown up and worked in the Otways for over 12 years, I know this landscape better than anyone.

As a Qualified Arborist, I don't just see a tree as something to cut down. Every job gets the right assessment, the right plan, and the right equipment — so you get a result you're proud of, without the stress.

We treat every property like it's our own."

What Sets Us Apart

You don't always have to have big toys to make a big impact.

With our specialised equipment focused on low environmental impact and quick turnaround, we do the hard work for you.

Expert Advice (Not Just Lopping)

We provide the right information from the start, focusing on long-term tree health and saving you money. You'll never be sold a removal when a prune will do the job.

The "Spotless Site" Guarantee

We take pride in our cleanup and are often called in to fix the half-finished jobs left behind by others. Every site is raked and blown down until it's cleaner than when we arrived — no exceptions.

Deep Local Knowledge

With over 12 years based in Colac and Apollo Bay, we understand the specific challenges of the Otway landscape — from high-wind storm cycles on the coast to dense gorse infestations inland. This isn't just a job to us, it's our backyard.

Service Areas

Serving Colac, The Otways, and The Coast.

As qualified arborists based in Apollo Bay, we provide tree removal, tree pruning, stump grinding, hedging, forestry mulching, property maintenance, waste removal, arborist reports, and 24/7 emergency storm response across Colac, Apollo Bay, Lorne, Winchelsea, Birregurra, Forrest, Beech Forest and the surrounding Otway region.
