About · Family-run arborists, based in Colac

A family business, led by a qualified arborist

Otway Tree Care is run by Tom Kleindienst, a qualified arborist with more than 12 years’ experience working on trees across Colac and the Otways.

  • Qualified arborist
  • $20M public liability + WorkCover
  • SWMS on every job
Why Otway Tree Care exists

Honest advice, the right gear, and a job finished properly

“I started Otway Tree Care to bring something different to this region — honest advice, qualified expertise, and a team that actually cares about the result. I don’t just see a tree as something to cut down. Every job gets the right assessment, the right plan and the right equipment.”

— Tom Kleindienst, founder

That still describes how we work. Most jobs start with Tom walking around the tree with you, explaining what he sees and what the options are. Sometimes the answer is a full removal. Sometimes it’s a reduction, some deadwood out, or — like the limb we turned into a nesting hollow — something you wouldn’t expect.

The view from work

Coast, farms, town and forest — often in the same week

Tom has a habit of taking a photo from the top of the tree. Between them, they’re a fair map of the region we work in.

How we work

What you can expect from us

Qualified judgement

Advice before cutting

We explain what’s happening with the tree and why before recommending any work. People trust decisions they can follow — and sometimes the right answer is less work, not more.

Local knowledge

We know the conditions

Salt wind on the coast, cypress windbreaks on the farms, steep and slippery blocks, and tall timber in wet gullies. Based in Colac, we work across all of it.

The right equipment

Gear matched to the site

Our own chipper, tracked stump grinder and loader-mounted mulcher, plus trucks to cart material away. When a job needs an elevated work platform or a crane, we bring one in.

A complete finish

Fixed price, safe work, clean site

A fixed-price quote before we start, a Safe Work Method Statement for every job, and a clean-up that’s part of the job — not an extra.

The crew

A small crew, and the next generation of climbers

Tom works alongside a small crew, including an apprentice arborist learning climbing, rigging and machine work on real Otways jobs.
