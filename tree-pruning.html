Why Choose Us

Why Proper Pruning is an Investment, Not an Expense.

Poor pruning — often called tree lopping in Colac and across the region — does more than just look bad. It can permanently damage a tree's health and create future hazards. When a tree is cut incorrectly, it responds with weak, rapid regrowth that is prone to snapping in Otway storms.

At Otway Tree Care, we follow Australian Standards for pruning (AS 4373-2007). Whether we are weight-reducing a heavy limb over your driveway or thinning a canopy to improve light flow, every cut is made with the tree's long-term health in mind.

We help you manage your landscape assets without the stress of future failures. And if a tree is beyond saving, our tree removal team will handle it safely and cleanly — giving you honest advice at every step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest Advice. Expert Answers.

Good pruning decisions start with good information. Here are the questions our clients ask most before booking a pruning assessment.

What is the best time of year to prune my trees?

This depends on the species. Many trees are best pruned during dormancy in winter, while others respond better to summer pruning. We provide expert advice based on the specific trees on your property.

Can you "top" my tree to make it shorter?

We strongly advise against "topping," as it leads to weak regrowth and tree decay. Instead, we use "reduction pruning," which manages the height while maintaining the tree's natural shape and strength.

Will pruning help my tree survive a storm?

Yes. By thinning the canopy and removing dead or over-extended limbs, we reduce wind resistance, significantly lowering the risk of limb failure during high winds.

What is the difference between "Lopping" and "Pruning"?

Lopping is the practice of cutting branches at random lengths, which causes stress. Pruning is a scientific approach where we cut to specific "nodes" to encourage proper healing.

Are you insured for work near my house?

Absolutely. We carry Public Liability insurance specifically tailored for high-risk arboricultural work, providing total peace of mind while we work near your assets.

Do I need to be home while you work?

Not necessarily. As long as we have clear access to the trees, we can complete the work and send you completion photos and reports via our digital app.

Can you provide a health report for my trees?

Yes. If we notice signs of pests, fungal infections, or structural decay while pruning, we can provide a formal arborist report so you have documented evidence to act on before it becomes a hazard.
