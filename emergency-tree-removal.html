Frequently Asked Questions

Honest Advice. Expert Answers.

In an emergency, you need fast, clear answers — not guesswork. Here are the most common questions we receive during storm events and urgent callouts.

How quickly can you get here?

In an emergency, we prioritise site safety. Our response time depends on your location and the current weather conditions, but we aim to have a crew dispatched immediately.

Will insurance cover the cost of emergency tree removal?

In most cases, if a tree has fallen on a covered structure (like a house or fence), insurance covers the removal. We provide the digital photos and formal arborist reports needed to assist with your claim.

Can you remove a tree from my roof without causing more damage?

Yes. We use "crane-style" lifting or specialised rigging to lift the weight off the structure vertically, rather than dragging it, which prevents further structural damage.

What should I do while waiting for the crew to arrive?

Stay well clear of the tree at all times. Do not attempt to move vehicles or enter any structure the tree is leaning on. If you suspect any utilities are involved, contact the relevant authority and keep everyone well back until our crew arrives.

Do you work in the dark or during active storms?

We work 24/7. However, if conditions are too dangerous (e.g., extreme wind or lightning), we may stabilise the site first and complete the full removal once it is safe for our crew to operate.

What is the cost of an emergency call-out?

Emergency rates reflect the immediate dispatch of specialised crews and machinery outside of business hours. We provide a clear estimate before work begins.

Can you help with trees blocking my driveway?

Yes. We prioritise access. If you are trapped or emergency vehicles cannot reach you, let us know immediately so we can prioritise clearing your access route.
