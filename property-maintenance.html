Why Choose Us

The Struggle of Acreage Management

Maintaining a large property in the Otway region is a constant battle against fast-growing vegetation and changing seasons. Without the right equipment, "weekend maintenance" quickly becomes an overwhelming struggle that eats into your free time or business operations.

At Otway Tree Care, we take the burden off your shoulders. We combine our specialised machinery fleet with a scheduled maintenance approach to manage fire fuel loads, clear fence lines, and maintain internal tracks.

We don't just visit and leave — we manage your land's health, safety, and long-term value, so you can enjoy your property without the constant pressure of maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest Advice. Expert Answers.

We believe in providing the right information upfront so you can make the best decisions for your trees and your property. Whether you’re concerned about local council permits or the safety of a hazardous removal, we’re here to provide the clear, professional guidance you need.

Do you offer fixed-price maintenance contracts?

Yes. After an initial site assessment, we can provide a fixed-price schedule for recurring visits so you can budget your property expenses with total certainty.

Can you manage a property while I am not there?

Absolutely. Many of our clients are absentee owners. We use an online app to track our jobs, allowing us to send you photos and completion reports the moment we leave the gate.

What equipment do you use for large-scale maintenance?

Depending on the job, we use everything from our low impact tracked machinery and forestry mulchers to specialised commercial mowers and long-reach hedging gear.

Do you handle weed control or just tree work?

We focus on "Vegetation Management," which includes the removal of woody weeds (Gorse, Blackberry) via mulching and manual clearing of overgrown areas.

Can you help with fire season preparation?

Yes. This is one of our core maintenance services. We focus on reducing the "fuel ladder" by thinning undergrowth and removing dead wood near structures.

Are you available for one-off "blitz" cleanups?

While we specialise in ongoing care, we frequently perform large-scale "reclamation" cleans for properties that have been neglected for several seasons.

How do you handle green waste?

We prefer to mulch on-site to return nutrients to your soil, but for formal estates, we can arrange full debris removal to keep the property looking manicured.
