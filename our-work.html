Our work · Projects from across the Otways

Real jobs, and how we approached them

Every tree and every site is different. These are real jobs from our own crew — what the problem was, how we handled it, and how it turned out.

Featured

A dead eucalyptus too dangerous to climb

A huge dead eucalypt stood on a hillside above homes, a driveway and the road. Climbing it wasn’t safe, so Tom worked from a hired spider lift instead.

More projects

Forrest · Tree removal over a house

A liquid amber that kept flooding the gutters

A towering liquid amber was blocking gutters, causing flooding and mould inside, and had already failed onto the house once. It came down with rigging over the roof and service line.

Otway region · Habitat creation

A dead limb kept as a home for wildlife

A dead limb was due to come down for firewood. After a chat with a regular client who loves birdlife, we carved a nesting hollow into it instead.

Otway hinterland · Hedge and windbreak removal

An old cypress windbreak makes way for a native garden

An overgrown cypress windbreak along a rural driveway was removed, chipped and ground out to make way for a native garden that’s easier to manage.

Otway region · Stump grinding

A stubborn stump turned into a parking pad

The owner had been trying to burn this stump out for years and needed the space for parking. We ground it out and built up a gravel pad.

Geelong · Urgent stump grinding

Stumps out before the drilling rig arrived

A builder needed stumps gone from a narrow side yard before foundation drilling started on the Monday. Big machines couldn’t get in; our grinder could.

Apollo Bay · Clearance pruning

Clearing a eucalyptus off the solar lights at Apollo Bay’s new roundabout

A eucalyptus at the new roundabout on Pascoe and Moore Streets was shading the solar panels on the street lights. We pruned it clear.
